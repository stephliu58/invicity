Our story

Built for people who are always moving

Whether you're a digital nomad, a frequent business traveler, or someone who simply refuses to stay in one place — Invicity was built for you.

We're building the world's first truly global professional community — curated, intimate, and designed around the reality that the most ambitious people don't stay still.

About  ·  The founder

Hi, I'm Stephanie — founder of Invicity.

I spent over three years in management consulting in the US, advising Fortune 500 companies on how to build better systems. Meanwhile, I was quietly building one of my own — a life stitched together across cities.

Here's the thing about your twenties: your people end up everywhere. After college, my best friends scattered across the world — San Francisco, New York, Stockholm, Barcelona, Hong Kong, and more. Suddenly my favorite hobby was going to the airport. I'd fly out on a Friday just to crash someone's Monday trivia night, be a plus-one at a party where I knew no one, or sign up for a dance class and completely humiliate myself. Worth it every time.

But here's what I didn't expect: the best nights weren't the Eiffel Tower or the must-see museum. They were the random Tuesdays when you start dancing with strangers and somehow end up at three more parties by 2am.

I've moved countries twice — from home to the US for college, then from the US to London — both times knowing no one. What I've learned is that a city is only as good as the people you know in it.

That's why I left consulting to build Invicity. We've already hosted Invicity Nights in London, with members flying in from other countries just to be there. And this is only the beginning.

If your group chat is already spread across three time zones, you're one of us.

Stephanie

Founder, Invicity · London

H. & S.

Two members met at an Invicity Wine Night in London — strangers seated at the same table who quickly became close friends. Within months, they were on a plane to Scotland together for a week-long trip.
